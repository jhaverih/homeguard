# Attenteve — Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

> **Status: draft, not legally reviewed.** This document is a starting point covering the categories of data Attenteve's app and platform actually collect, based on the current product. **It has not been reviewed by an attorney and must not be published or relied upon until counsel confirms it accurately reflects your data practices and complies with the privacy laws applicable to where Attenteve actually operates and where its customers/vendors are located** (state privacy laws in the US vary significantly, e.g. California, Virginia, Colorado, and others each have their own requirements; this draft is deliberately written to avoid citing a specific state law by name until that determination is made).

**Last updated: July 15, 2026**

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Attenteve ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Attenteve app or website as a customer or vendor (collectively, the "Service").

## 2. Information We Collect

- **Account information**: name, email address, phone number, password (stored encrypted).
- **Property information** (customers): home address, city, state, zip code, and details you provide about your home.
- **Payment information**: processed and stored by our payment processor, Stripe; Attenteve does not store your full card number.
- **Service history**: records of inspections, service requests, appointments, photos taken during inspections or job completion, notes and findings recorded by vendors, and chat messages with our AI maintenance assistant (Eve AI).
- **Home monitoring data** (if enabled): sensor readings (e.g. leak detection, temperature, humidity) from connected third-party hardware (Yolink), and alert history.
- **Vendor information**: business details, trade licenses/certifications, insurance documentation, background check results, and payout account information (processed by Stripe Connect).
- **Location information**: a vendor's approximate location while en route to a job, shared with the customer for that job only.
- **Device information**: push notification token, device type, and app version, used to deliver notifications and maintain service reliability.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to: provide and improve the Service; match customers with vendors; process payments and payouts; send appointment, alert, and account notifications; provide customer support (including through Eve AI); maintain the security and integrity of the platform; and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. How We Share Information

- **With vendors**: a customer's property address, service request details, and relevant photos/notes are shared with the vendor assigned to a job, as necessary to perform the service.
- **With customers**: a vendor's name, photo, and approximate location while en route are shared with the customer for that job.
- **With service providers**: we share information with vendors we use to operate the platform, including Stripe (payments and payouts), our cloud hosting provider, and Yolink (home monitoring hardware/data, for customers who enable that feature).
- **For legal reasons**: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Attenteve, our users, or the public.
- **We do not sell your personal information.**

## 5. Data Retention

We retain account and service history information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, tax, dispute-resolution, or legitimate business purposes. You may request deletion of your account as described in Section 6.

## 6. Your Choices & Rights

You can review and update your account information in the app at any time. The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it.
