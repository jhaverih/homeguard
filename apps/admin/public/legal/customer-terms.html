5. Booking, Cancellation & No-Show Policy

5.1 Payment method verification

This hold is automatically released — it is separate from, and does not count toward, any charge for the completed service or any cancellation fee below.

5.2 Cancelling a scheduled service

  • More than 24 hours before your scheduled appointment time: Free. No cancellation fee.
  • Less than 24 hours before your scheduled appointment time: A minimum cancellation fee of $25 applies. This compensates your Vendor for the time they set aside for your appointment.
  • No-show (the Vendor arrives at your home at the scheduled time and is unable to reach you or gain access): You will be charged the full price of the service.

5.3 Why this policy exists

Vendors reserve time in their schedule for your appointment and often turn away other work to do so. Late cancellations and no-shows leave that time unpaid. This tiered policy — free cancellation with reasonable notice, a modest fee for late cancellations, full charge for no-shows — mirrors standard practice across home-service and appointment-based platforms and is intended to be fair to both sides: you keep full flexibility to cancel for free with a day's notice, and your Vendor isn't left uncompensated for reserved time they can no longer fill.

5.4 How charges are applied

Cancellation fees and no-show charges are billed to the payment method on file for the affected service request, separately from the payment-verification hold described above.
