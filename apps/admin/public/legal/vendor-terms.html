4. Payment Method Verification & Customer Cancellations

4.1 Payment method verification

When a customer books a service, Attenteve places a small, temporary authorization hold on their payment method to confirm it's valid before you're dispatched. This is separate from, and does not reduce, your payout for the completed job or any cancellation/no-show compensation below.

4.2 Customer cancellations

  • More than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment: The customer may cancel free of charge. No compensation is owed to you for this appointment.
  • Less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment: The customer is charged a minimum cancellation fee of $25, and you receive 100% of this fee — no platform fee is deducted from it — since it exists specifically to compensate you for the time you set aside for the job.
  • No-show (you arrive at the scheduled time and are unable to reach the customer or gain access to the property): The customer is charged the full price of the service, and you receive 100% of this charge — no platform fee is deducted — following the same reasoning: this compensation exists to make you whole for a job slot that was reserved and then lost.

4.3 Why this policy exists

This policy protects the time you reserve for scheduled jobs. Customers get full flexibility to cancel for free with a day's notice, while late cancellations and no-shows — which leave a slot in your schedule that's too late to refill — are compensated in full rather than absorbed as a loss on your end.

4.4 Your responsibilities

To keep this policy fair to customers, arrive at the scheduled time and make a good-faith effort to reach the customer (per Attenteve's standard on-site check-in process) before a visit is recorded as a no-show.
